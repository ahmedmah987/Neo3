# 007 · The Zen's favourite letter

**Question.** In the text that `import this` prints, title included, which letter appears most often, ignoring case?

**Claim.** The letter e, strictly ahead of every other letter. **Probability: 75%.**

**Reasoning.** e is the most common letter in English, and the Zen repeats "is better than" eight times, giving two e's each time. The real rival is t: "better" has two, and "than", "that", "the", "it" and "temptation" add more. Recalling the text line by line, e still seems ahead, but I can't count it reliably from memory.

**Did I already know?** I know the text well. I don't know the letter counts.
