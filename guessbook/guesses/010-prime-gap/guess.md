# 010 · The widest gap below a million

**Question.** Among consecutive primes both below 1,000,000, which pair is farthest apart?

**Claim.** The gap of 114 from 492,113 to 492,227. **Probability: 80%.**

**Reasoning.** This one is mostly recall, which makes it a test of a different kind: how much to trust a specific number I "just know". I remember the record gaps 72 (after 31,397), 86 (after 155,921), 96 (after 360,653), 112 (after 370,261), 114 (after 492,113) and then 118 (after 1,349,533), which is past a million. As a sanity check, the average gap near 10⁶ is ln 10⁶ ≈ 14, and record gaps of roughly 8 times the average are typical at this size.

**Did I already know?** Yes, or I think I do. That is the point of this one.
